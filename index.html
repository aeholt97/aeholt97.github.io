About

I research information disorders on social media, with a particular focus on Europe. Using primarily quantitative and computational methods — network analysis, natural language processing and large-scale platform data — my dissertation examines how misinformation has changed over time in relation to offline events, shifts in platform moderation practices, and technological developments.

I am based at DATALAB – Center for Digital Social Research at Aarhus University, where I am part of the Social Media Influence project (2023–2028, PI Anja Bechmann), studying social media as infrastructures of influence in national populations with a focus on false information.

In spring 2026 I was a visiting scholar at the Annenberg School for Communication, University of Pennsylvania, hosted by Professor Sandra González-Bailón, working on network analyses of conspiratorial Facebook content.
